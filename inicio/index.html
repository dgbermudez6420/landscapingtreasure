A few things
you might be wondering.

What exactly is this collection?

Landscaping Treasure is a collection of garden design references, visual inspiration, and practical planning notes. Three companion guides are complete: the 24-plant Ornamental Plant Catalog, Garden Lighting Guide, and Plant Combination Guide. The Front Yard & Facade Design Ideas and Complete Pest Control Guide remain proposed. The material is a visual reference to adapt, rather than a set of site-specific architectural or construction drawings.

How will I receive the material?

The planned format is a digital collection, with no physical shipment. Review the final download format, delivery method, and access terms on Hotmart before purchasing. This page is currently a private product and landing-page preview.

Can I view it on my phone or tablet?

The proposed collection is intended to be easy to browse on a phone, tablet, or computer. The sample pages on this landing page work on all three. Final delivery files and access still need to be tested before launch.

Do the ideas include exact measurements?

The planned notes offer suggested space requirements and layout guidance, not surveyed measurements or permit-ready plans. Measure your own property and have structural, drainage, irrigation, electrical, or other technical work reviewed by a qualified local professional.

Will every garden work in my climate?

No single plant palette suits every location. Use the concepts as inspiration, then check local hardiness, heat, sunlight, soil, rainfall, water restrictions, mature plant size, and invasive-species guidance. A local nursery, extension service, or landscape professional can help adapt your choices.

Can I use it when planning a client’s garden?

The concepts can help you explore styles and discuss ideas. The final product license will need to define permitted professional use before sale. The designs still need to be adapted to each site, and this preview does not grant rights to redistribute or resell a future collection.
